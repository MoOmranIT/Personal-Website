Growth is rarely the result of doing more of everything. It usually begins with identifying which opportunities deserve attention and which ones do not.

## Start With the Market

A useful growth strategy begins by understanding the market, the customer, and the problem the business is actually positioned to solve.

## Focus the Effort

Clear priorities help teams concentrate resources on the opportunities that can create the strongest long-term value.

- Understand the market before scaling.
- Choose a clear growth priority.
- Align execution with the strategic direction.
